## 2. Detailed Description (Formatted for Chrome Web Store)

🎓 **StudyTube is the Glass-Box Bandwidth Optimization Engine engineered for global learners, digital nomads, and hotspot streamers.**

Whether you are a STEM student studying over a metered mobile connection, a remote professional tethering to an expensive international travel eSIM (Airalo, Holafly), an ISP customer managing monthly data caps (Xfinity, Cox), or a gym/cafe manager running continuous background audio, standard YouTube silently drains gigabytes of data you never asked for.

YouTube default settings either auto-jump to heavy 1080p/4K streams (burning 2GB–4GB/hr) or degrade down to blurry 144p/240p (making math formulas, blackboard equations, and code syntax unreadable). Even worse, YouTube pre-buffers hundreds of megabytes of unseen sidebar recommendation thumbnails, Shorts, and hover previews.

StudyTube solves this with **Smart, Context-Aware Data Conservation**: keep visuals crisp and readable while eliminating background waste.

---

### 🚀 Key Capabilities:

1. **💡 Glass-Box AI Content Advice**:
   • Automatically identifies video genre (STEM Whiteboard, Programming/IDE, Verbal Seminar, or Background Audio).
   • Proactively advises the mathematically optimal playback mode with full transparency.

2. **Context-Aware Playback Modes**:
   • 📐 **Board Mode (720p HD)**: Engineered for STEM lectures. Delivers razor-sharp clarity for handwritten math, chemical formulas, and whiteboard text while cutting bandwidth by ~45%.
   • 💻 **Code Mode (720p/1080p)**: Ensures sharp monospaced fonts for terminal commands, IDE syntax, and programming tutorials.
   • 🗣️ **Talk Mode (480p)**: Optimized for verbal talks, conferences, and slides. Conserves ~65% data with full vocal and slide outline clarity.
   • 🎧 **Audio-First Mode**: Suppresses video rendering to save ~85% data for background gym music, podcasts, or low-connectivity commutes.

3. **🛡️ The Zero-Waste Shield**:
   • Suppresses background prefetching of heavy sidebar recommendations and thumbnails.
   • Blocks silent hover video previews and background Shorts chunk streaming.
   • Centers your video for a clean, distraction-free study theater.

4. **📊 Verifiable Stream Telemetry & Receipts**:
   • Tracks actual megabytes streamed vs. megabytes conserved via the browser's native `PerformanceObserver` API.
   • Instant 1-click **Shareable Savings Receipts** for Discord, WhatsApp, or study groups.

5. **⏸️ Background Tab Auto-Pause**:
   • Automatically halts video streaming when you switch tabs to take notes or write code, preventing silent background hotspot drain.
